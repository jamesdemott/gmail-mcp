gmail-mcp privacy policy

Last updated October 6, 2026.

gmail-mcp is a personal tool used only by its developer, James De Mott, with his own Gmail account.

What it accesses

With the developer's consent, the app reads Gmail messages and creates drafts in his account through the Gmail API.

Where data goes

The app runs on the developer's own computer. Sign-in tokens are stored only on that computer. The app has no server of its own and does not collect, sell, or share Gmail data. Message content is passed only to the AI assistant the developer is using, at his request.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
